# Every button, rated by how often people press it

Rule: **press it most sessions → sidebar or the screen itself. Weekly or rarer → profile menu or Settings.**
Score = expected presses in a typical working session (1 = once a month, 5 = many times a session).
Learned from ResearchRabbit (2 top-level tabs, project picker and account tucked in a corner,
everything else in context) and from Elicit/SciSpace (one big action, nothing competing with it).

## Result

weekly competes with Screening and Reader for attention, and the phone drawer no longer
squeezes the navigation into three rows.
